# Wallabag

Read saved Wallabag articles on your CrossPoint reader by downloading them as EPUB files.

Browse Unread, Starred, Archived, and All articles, search by keyword, and move through paginated results. Configure your self-hosted or hosted Wallabag server, API client credentials, and account in the device WebUI.

Downloaded EPUBs are saved to `/Wallabag/` on the SD card. Credentials are stored on the SD card; keep it secure.
